VOICE GUIDE — style only
Tone and what the world cares about

Grim, grounded, tired. Faith, coin, and smoke run everything. Institutions are pious in public and corrupt up close: bribed clergy, land-buying guilds, officials who stamp forms and look away. Worth is measured in visible piety, paperwork, and debt. Furnaces, steam and clockwork are replacing hands. People endure with gallows humor and small kindnesses. Hope is small and expensive.

Rhythm and detail
Use short declarative sentences. For tension, break into fragments: "No sound. No warning."
Follow short beats with one longer sentence that lands the image.
Give one smell, one sound, one texture (wet stone, rust, tallow, soot). Don't stack adjectives.
Show character through physical tells, never exposition.
Flavor text runs 1–3 sentences. Events run 3–6. End on a quiet, wry, or ominous beat.
Dialogue: clipped, a little bitter.
Magic

Magic is suspect, regulated, and costly. Licensed casting needs writs. The rest happens in back rooms. Every spell has a price: blisters, blood, lost sleep, a debt, a mark on the skin. Make it physical: heat in the palms, static in the teeth. It's never wondrous without a catch.

Danger, violence, corruption
Danger shows up as wrongness before it shows up as a monster: silence that waits, animals moving together.
Violence is fast, ugly, and consequential. Describe the aftermath more than the blow, and never cheer it.
Corruption is quiet and procedural: a fee, a form, a name struck from a list. Don't explain or moralize.
Naming patterns
People: short, hard given names and trade-compound surnames (tool or metal + object). Criminals get quoted nicknames. Clergy and healers get an honorific (Brother/Sister/Mother/Deacon) plus a soft given name.
Places: plain-English compounds that name a sin, a trade, or a past disaster. Streets end in Row, Lane, Ward, Yard, Slope. Taverns get ironic or over-blessed names.
Institutions: euphemistic bureaucracy, like Office of…, Registry, Order, Guild, Company. The name sounds helpful, and the work isn't.
Items: material + function, labeled like shop stock, often with a price. Darker items get euphemisms.
Never appears
Modern slang, memes, brands, or real-world names
